About Memory Game

Memory is a fun and fast-paced card-matching game designed to challenge your recall skills. The game is timed, so the quicker you match all the cards, the better your score!

Memory is a great way to test your memory and improve your cognitive skills. It's also a fun way to pass the time and challenge yourself to beat your previous scores.

The game consists of 6 pairs, making a total of 12 cards. Your goal is to flip the cards, find all matching pairs, and beat the clock.

Your results are color-coded based on the number of flips it takes to find a match:

  • Green Square: Match found on the first flip.
  • Purple Square: Match found on the second flip.
  • Blue Square: Match found on the third flip.
  • Yellow Square: Match found on the fourth flip.
  • Red Square: Match found on the fifth flip.
  • Black Square: Match found after five flips.

Good luck, and see how fast your memory can match.
